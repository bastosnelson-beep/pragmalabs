01 / AI maturity audit

Know where you stand, and where to start.

Problems we solve
1

No overall picture

You know AI can transform your business, but no one really knows where the company stands, or where to begin.

2

Scattered initiatives

Each department tests its own tools, often outside any framework: budget spread thin, data exposed, no consolidated results.

3

Investing without knowing the return

Without priorities or indicators, it is impossible to prioritise, convince leadership or measure results.

Our solutions

A diagnostic across four dimensions

Team skills, processes, data, and infrastructure & security: a score for each, compared with what is achievable for you.

Interviews with leadership and teams

We start from your reality, not from a generic questionnaire.

An inventory of AI usage

Which tools, used by whom, with what data and at what cost, including undeclared usage.

High-potential use cases

The most time-consuming, tedious or high-impact processes, assessed and ranked.

Risk assessment

Confidentiality, GDPR and AI Act compliance, vendor dependency, access security.

A costed roadmap

Quick wins, priority projects, budget, expected gains and tracking indicators, presented to the leadership team.

What you get
  • A maturity score for each dimension
  • A map of risks and opportunities
  • A prioritised, costed roadmap

02 / Method & training

Your whole team at the same level of AI mastery.

Problems we solve
1

Everyone tinkers with AI on their own

Individual habits with no shared rules: results vary from person to person and nothing is captured or built on.

2

Unpredictable results

Without understanding how a model works or giving it the right context, you get generic output or errors. Trust collapses.

3

AI amplifies disorder

Without organised information or routines, AI adds to the chaos instead of reducing it. Tools are abandoned within weeks.

Our solutions

A shared foundation of understanding

How a model reasons, context, hallucinations: understand in order to master, from leadership to teams.

Rules for working with AI

Frame before you execute, one conversation per objective, never delegate clarity of thought.

A second brain for each employee

A knowledge space structured by use, which the AI reads as permanent context.

Short, sustainable routines

Session wrap-up, weekly review, monthly clean-up: the system stays alive without effort.

The amplification standard

Shared rules, best practices and a skills library that bring everyone to the same level.

Role-based tracks, on your real cases

Leadership, managers, teams: workshops on your own files. Also meets the AI Act's AI literacy obligation.

What you get
  • A shared AI culture
  • A documented usage standard
  • Self-sufficient teams

03 / Process mapping & skillification

Turn your know-how into processes that AI executes.

Problems we solve
1

Know-how stays in people's heads

A few experts carry the methods: they cannot be delegated or improved, and the company is exposed when people leave.

2

Isolated automations

AI is plugged into one-off tasks rather than complete workflows: the gains stay marginal.

3

Automating too early

A process that was never stabilised ends up breaking, costs maintenance, and the team disengages.

Our solutions

A complete process map

By frequency (daily, weekly, monthly, on trigger) and by function: acquisition, sales, delivery, support, administration.

Value-based prioritisation

Each process is scored on time spent, tedium and business impact. The most valuable go first.

Method captured with your experts

Steps, quality criteria, examples, pitfalls: we capture what makes your way of working valuable.

Skillification

Each method becomes a skill: a single process sheet the AI executes faithfully. Documentation and execution become one.

Progressive automation

Manual, supervised, semi-automatic, then autonomous. Only on proven processes, with a named owner and alerts.

Rollout across the company

A shared skills library: every department benefits from the others' processes.

What you get
  • A map of your processes
  • A library of skills
  • Hours freed up, measured process by process

04 / AI strategy & infrastructure

Secure, sovereign AI with controlled costs.

Problems we solve
1

Scattered data

Emails, spreadsheets, ERP, CRM, documents: without a single source, AI consumes a lot, answers slowly and contradicts itself.

2

Sensitive data out of control

Who sends what, to which provider, kept for how long? Credentials and keys circulate outside any framework.

3

Unpredictable bills, vendor lock-in

Spiralling costs, a single model, terms that change overnight.

Our solutions

Unified, connected data

A single source of truth for each piece of data, and connectors to your tools: ERP, CRM, office suites, document repositories.

Confidentiality by design

Contractual Zero Data Retention (ZDR), European or sovereign hosting, local models for the most sensitive data.

The right model for each task

Chosen by sensitivity and complexity, with no vendor lock-in. The model changes, your system stays.

Cost control

Flat-rate plans rather than open-ended usage, budgets per use case, consumption tracking, tools enabled only when needed.

Access and credential security

Secrets vault, least-privilege access, bounded permissions for agents, no exposed keys.

Monitoring and continuous evolution

Tracking of usage, quality and costs. Your infrastructure adopts new models without starting over.

What you get
  • A sovereign infrastructure
  • Predictable AI costs
  • A model-independent architecture
