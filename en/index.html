Overview

PragmaLabs is an Applied AI Lab. We rebuild the core processes of the organizations that matter, making them AI-native, with a measurable impact on their operating income.

STEP 1

Map the processes

Together with our clients' teams, we analyse their key business workflows to pinpoint recurring friction points, handing over our method so they can redesign these processes as agent systems that execute the work end to end.

STEP 2

Industrialize

We deploy these systems into production, with rigorous evaluations and a continuous improvement loop that keeps these processes alive within their ecosystem.

STEP 3

Own

We build a sovereign AI asset tailored to the company, one it keeps owning and that keeps compounding its value. Data sovereignty, process sovereignty, while staying agnostic to the AI models used.
