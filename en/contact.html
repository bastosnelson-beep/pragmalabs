FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Find answers to the most common questions.

How does a project with Pragma4 get started?

We begin with a free initial workshop to understand your needs and identify automation opportunities. Then, based on the first audits, we define a clear action plan together, with precise deliverables. Development runs in short sprints with regular check-ins, so the solution matches your expectations exactly.

How long does a project take?

Timelines depend on the project's complexity. A simple automation can be up and running in 1 to 2 weeks. For more ambitious projects, such as a custom AI application or connecting your different tools, allow 4 to 8 weeks. A detailed schedule is shared with your project team from the start.

How does pricing work?

After the analysis phase, you receive a precise, detailed proposal with no commitment. We bill either at a fixed price or on a time-and-materials basis, depending on your request. Full transparency on our offers and on every phase of the project is one of our core values.

How do you ensure data security?

Security is at the heart of our approach. We use industry-standard encryption protocols. Your data belongs to you, and your sovereignty over it is a key principle for us, in line with the GDPR. We follow your organization's access rules and the privileges granted to each user. By default, your data is never used to train third-party models. If you choose a model that does, you do so with full knowledge: throughout the project, we advise you on Zero Data Retention (ZDR), security and data anonymization.

Do we need technical skills on our side?

No, no technical skills are required. We handle all the technical work, the documentation, and the handover to your teams so they can use the tools. Our goal is to make you self-sufficient in using, understanding and evolving the methods we put in place.

Do you offer support after delivery?

Yes. Every project includes 6 weeks of support after go-live, the first two with enhanced, hands-on assistance. We handle maintenance and updates, and remain available for any questions. Extended support contracts are also available for ongoing assistance.

What ROI can I expect?

ROI is defined with you during the scoping phase. It can cover time saved, value created, cost savings or new contracts won. Our clients see significant growth in the weeks following the adoption of the methods and systems we put in place. Adoption is fast, and the return on investment is usually visible within the first weeks. During scoping, we estimate the expected gains together and define the indicators that will measure them.

What sets Pragma4 apart from AI tools you can subscribe to directly?

Mainstream AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Dust, Microsoft Copilot…) are powerful but generic: they are not tailored to your processes. Successful automation rests first on method and on mastery of your data; technology (AI models, automation tools) then amplifies it. Through its method, Pragma4 makes you self-sufficient. We always give you the keys to understanding, and we build custom solutions integrated with your existing tools, with automated processes and full control over quality and consistency.
